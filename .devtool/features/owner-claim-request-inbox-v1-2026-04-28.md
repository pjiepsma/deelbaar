# Owner claim request inbox (v1)

Create a clear inbox flow for ownership requests and decisions.

## Scope
- Show claim requests with status and notes.
- Decision actions for approve/reject in moderated contexts.
- Audit visibility for decisions and timestamps.

## Acceptance criteria
- Pending, approved, and rejected requests are clearly visible.
- Decision updates are persisted with status and notes.
- Ownership transfer behavior is correct after approval.
- Users receive clear feedback for claim outcomes.
