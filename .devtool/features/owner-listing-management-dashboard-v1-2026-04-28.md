# Owner listing management dashboard (v1)

Provide an account dashboard for owners to manage their listings lifecycle.

## Scope
- List owner listings with draft/live state.
- Quick actions for edit, publish, and unpublish.
- Visibility of listing status and basic health indicators.

## Acceptance criteria
- Owner sees only listings they own.
- Publish/unpublish transitions are enforced server-side.
- Unauthorized listing actions are blocked.
- Listing status updates reflect immediately in owner dashboard.
