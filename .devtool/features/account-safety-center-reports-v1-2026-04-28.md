# Account safety center + reports (v1)

Give users a dedicated account area for report history and status visibility.

## Scope
- List reports created by the current user.
- Show report status lifecycle and resolution notes.
- Route users to relevant listing/review context from reports.

## Acceptance criteria
- User can view only their own reports in account.
- Report statuses (open/in-review/resolved/dismissed) render correctly.
- Resolution notes are visible when available.
- Safety center reflects backend updates without stale data.
