# Account follow management center

Add a centralized account view for listing and area follows.

## Scope
- List own follows (listing + area).
- Follow/unfollow actions from account context.
- Active/inactive state visibility.

## Acceptance criteria
- User sees only own follow records.
- User can unfollow and refollow listing and area follows.
- Follow state changes are persisted and reflected in UI.
- Follow actions align with notification subscription behavior.
